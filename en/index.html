The entire sales conversation, from the first "hello" to the signed contract. This isn't a persuasion technique: it's guiding method — ask better questions, listen more, read what wasn’t said, and end every meeting with a date scheduled.

New here? Discovery conversation is the meeting where you investigate the problem before proposing anything. Funnel is the path from "I have a concern" to "I signed." Objection is any reason, stated or unstated, for not moving forward. Each term is defined in the course the first time it appears.
